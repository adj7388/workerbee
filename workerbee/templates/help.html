This web app can help players find words for an NYT Spelling Bee puzzle. Just enter the letters from a Spelling Bee game (or make up your own letters).

The "required letter" is the letter in the yellow tile in the middle of the Spelling Bee puzzle screen.

The "other letters" are the other six letters around the yellow one.

Unfortunately, the dictionary for this app is huge, and many (most?) of the words it includes are NOT valid Spelling Bee words. Sorry about that. You'll have to dig through a lot of irrelevant words to find the valid ones.

If you know of a better (open source) dictionary option, with fewer words, I would love to know about it. Contact me: a d j 7 3 8 8 at proton.me (remove the spaces, obviously).
