What is this?

This web app can help players find words for the NYT Spelling Bee puzzle. Just enter the letters from Spelling Bee (or make up your own letters).

Those who do the Spelling Bee will know what this means. If you're confused, this application is not for you.

What do the fields on the Home screen mean?

Again, IYKYK.

The "required letter" is the letter in the middle, yellow tile on the Spelling Bee screen.

The "other letters" are the six letters around the middle, yellow one.

Look up words using. What's that?

It's annoying to find some obscure word, then have to go to some dictionary site and look it up. You can use this option to choose which dictionary the words will link to, instantly providing the definition (in most cases).

My apologies for the irrelevant words

Unfortunately, the dictionary for this app is huge, and the vast majority of the words the application finds for a given set of letters are NOT valid Spelling Bee words. You'll have to dig through a lot of irrelevant words to find the ones you want. Sorry about that.
